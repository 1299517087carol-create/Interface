Overview

The Red Dot Award: Design Concept represents the premier international benchmark for discovering nascent design possibilities, imaginative student prototypes, and research grade physical innovations. Established in 2005 and headquartered in Singapore, the award acknowledges ideas that demonstrate exceptional foresight, ergonomic utility, ecological consciousness, and transformative technological integration.
